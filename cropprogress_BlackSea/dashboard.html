Agronomic Weather Reality: Why 2026 Harvest Pace Lags Far Behind Previous Years

In 2024, an extraordinarily dry, hot late summer allowed rapid combine harvesting without pause (corn dried down naturally to 12-14% moisture on the stalk, reaching 37% by early October). In 2026, persistent heavy September rains across the grain belt (ECMWF recorded 107.6 mm in Poltava, 89.7 mm in Chernihiv, 74.5 mm in Kirovohrad) caused waterlogged soils and high grain moisture, halting combines and dragging corn pace down to just 6.5% and sunflower to 48.6%.
