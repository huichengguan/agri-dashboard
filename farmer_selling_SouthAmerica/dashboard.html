ℹ️ Primary Sources & Whole Brazil Methodology

Brazil: Whole Country (Brasil Total) Brazil has no federal agency conducting weekly forward commercialization surveys. The national composite integrates state producer agency data (IMEA MT ~29%, DERAL PR ~15%, EMATER RS ~14%) weighted by official CONAB production sizes and calibrated to industry benchmarks (Safras & Mercado / AgRural / CONAB Supply Balances).
Brazil: Mato Grosso (IMEA) & Paraná (DERAL) Official primary monthly farmer surveys from IMEA (Mato Grosso) and weekly conjunctural surveys from DERAL (Paraná). Cotton strictly isolates Algodão em Pluma (Cotton Lint).
Argentina: SAGyP Official Official weekly commercialization bulletins published by the Secretaría de Agricultura, Ganadería y Pesca (SAGyP) under DJVE / Embarques, aggregating exporter purchases and domestic milling/crushing acquisitions.
