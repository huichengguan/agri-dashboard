ℹ️ Primary Sources, Extraction Yields & Methodology

1. Grain Purchasing Pipeline (Exporters vs. Crushers) SAGyP Compras y DJVE weekly bulletins segregate purchases by destination: Compras de la Industria (raw beans acquired by domestic crushers) and Sector Exportador (grain purchased for direct unprocessed export), detailing priced contracts vs floating unfixed commitments.
2. Industrial Crushing Throughput (SAGyP Molienda) Official monthly census from the Argentine Undersecretariat of Agricultural Markets (Evolución de la Molienda de Oleaginosas), tracking actual monthly seed volume processed in crushing mills and physical volumes of unrefined oils and pellets produced.
3. Agronomic Extraction Yields & Byproducts Standard Argentine industrial processing yields calibrated to CIARA & SAGyP specifications:
• Soybeans: 78.5% Soybean Meal (SBM) + 18.5% Crude Soybean Oil (SBO) + 3.0% loss.
• Sunflower: 42.5% Sunflower Oil (SFO) + 49.0% Sunflower Meal (SFM) + 8.5% loss.
• Why is SBM & SBO % Pace Equal? Crushers buy whole soybeans containing meal and oil simultaneously; hence feedstock coverage progresses in lockstep. To compare physical volumes, select Contracted Volume (MMT) or Actual Industrial Production (MMT).
4. Historical Survey Windows & Data Integrity In older seasons (prior to 2021-22), farmer forward selling in South America rarely occurred 18-24 months in advance; official agencies (IMEA / SAGyP) historically began surveys 8-12 months before harvest. Furthermore, once an old crop reached ~95-98% commercialization, agencies concluded the survey for that safra. We preserve authentic official points without synthetic interpolation.
