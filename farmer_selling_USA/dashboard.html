ℹ️ USA Institutional Methodology & Data Provenance

1. Historical Ground Truth (USDA NASS & ERS) Post-harvest marketing distributions are grounded in official monthly surveys of ~1,900 commercial grain elevators by USDA NASS (Agricultural Prices and ERS supply & disappearance yearbooks), calibrated to quarterly on-farm disappearance checkpoints (Grain Stocks).
2. Pre-Harvest Forward Engine (CFTC & FAS) Because elevators are not legally mandated to report cash contracts, forward commercialization (Jan–Aug pre-harvest) is modeled from weekly changes in CFTC Commercial (Producer/Merchant) short hedging positions and USDA FAS weekly export commitments.
3. Physical Volume Conversion Standardized to official USDA production sizes. 1 bu corn = 56 lbs (0.0254 MMT); 1 bu soybeans/wheat = 60 lbs (0.0272 MMT). User overrides for active seasons can be specified in data/usa_pace_overrides.json.
