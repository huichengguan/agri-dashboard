1. Leading Indicator for EIA Monthly: Official EIA Biofuel Monthly reports feedstock inputs with a ~60-day lag. These Kpler vessel discharges represent immediate physical inventory hitting refinery tanks today.

2. EPA D4 RIN Generation: Foreign UCO and Tallow flows directly support D4 Biomass-Based Diesel RIN generation. High import volumes depress D4 RIN prices, while shipping disruptions tighten domestic soybean oil crush demand.
