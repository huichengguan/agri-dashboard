Official Data Provenance & Accounting Notes (SAGyP / Secretaría de Energía / Argus Media Issue 26_5):
  • Accounting Identities (100% Reconciled): Ending Stocks for month t equal Beginning Stocks reported on the 1st of month t+1. Soybeans Delivered to Storage = Beginning Stocks + Delivered − Total Demand = Ending Stocks. SBM & SBO Domestic Use = Beginning Stocks + Production + Imports − Exports − Ending Stocks.
  • Marketing Year (MY): Argentina Soybean Marketing Year runs April – March (MY beginning 1 April). Switch Year Basis above between Calendar Year (Jan–Dec) and Marketing Year (Apr–Mar).
  • Dec 2020 (MY 2020/21) Crush Anomaly (846,919 t): Official SAGyP crush dropped to 846,919 t in Dec 2020 (with SBM production at 567,643 t and SBO production at 162,319 t) due to the 20-day nationwide Argentine oilseed workers & port grain inspectors strike (FTCIODyARA / SOEA / URGARA), before rebounding to 3,228,878 t in Jan 2021.
  • Historical Note (Dec 2017 – Feb 2018): In Jan and Feb 2018, SAGyP reported 0 for total off-farm Beginning Stocks (causing Argus's residual Delivered to Storage formula in Dec 2017 to be negative), whereas Beginning Stocks (Crushers) and all Crushed, Exports, SBM, and SBO series were reported without interruption.
  • Preliminary Sep 2026 Stock: Off-farm Soybeans Beginning Stocks for Oct 1, 2026 (= Sep 2026 Ending Stocks) is officially reported at 18,596,977 t (18.60 mn t); full Sep 2026 S&D flows are pending the next monthly release.
