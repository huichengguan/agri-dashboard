  • 249-Month High-Frequency Coverage (Jan 2006–Sep 2026): Expand any year column header ([+]) or switch to Monthly Matrix (By Year) or Monthly Seasonality Explorer to inspect official monthly series: (1) ANP Open Data monthly Anhydrous & Hydrous Ethanol production by State/Region (Jan 2012–Sep 2026, 177 months); (2) ANP/ABIOVE monthly Biodiesel production, auction/contract deliveries, Diesel B distributor sales & Diesel A imports (Jan 2006–Aug 2026, 248 months); (3) ABIOVE/SECEX (ComexStat) monthly export volumes, FOB values & average prices for Soybeans, Soybean Meal, Soybean Oil & Corn (Jan 2023–Sep 2026, plus 2007–2012 SBM exports); and (4) IBGE SIDRA Table 6588 (LSPA) monthly systematic crop production & area surveys (Sep 2006–Sep 2026, 241 months).
  • Mandatory Disclosure of Derived Figures ([EST]): Where monthly SBO biodiesel feedstock is derived from official monthly ANP biodiesel output × official annual ABIOVE SBO feedstock share (× 0.88 t/m³ density), or where annual Corn Ethanol grain grind, DDGS, and Corn Oil are converted from official CONAB Corn Ethanol production using standard CONAB/UNEM technical yields (440 L ethanol, 310 kg DDGS, and 18 kg corn oil per tonne of corn), cells are highlighted in orange with [EST] tooltips and tagged in estimated_fields. Unreported monthly cells remain -- (zero silent interpolation).
