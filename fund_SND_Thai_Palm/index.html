Official Data Provenance & Accounting Notes (Thailand Palm Oil S&D):
  • Source & Methodology: Compiled from official Thai Palm Oil statistics released by the Department of Internal Trade (DIT), Office of Agricultural Economics (OAE), and Energy Policy and Planning Office (EPPO). 100% official reported numbers, zero interpolation.
  • Accounting Identities (100% Reconciled): Starting Stocks for month t equals Ending Stocks of month t-1. Total Supply = Starting Stocks + Production + Imports. Total Demand = Exports + Total Domestic Consumption. Ending Stocks = Total Supply − Total Demand + Balancing Figure (where balancing figures reconcile official stock adjustments in 2024).
  • Thailand Biodiesel (BD) Mandates: Thailand mandates blend ratios for palm methyl ester (B100) into diesel fuel:
    • 2023 – 2024: B7 mandate in effect throughout the period.
    • 2025: B5 mandate temporarily implemented to balance domestic CPO supply and manage local price dynamics.
    • 2026: B5 mandate continued through February, transitioning back to B7 from March 2026 onwards.
  • Coverage & Latest Status: Full 12-month series available for 2023, 2024, and 2025. 2026 covers January through July actual reported flows, with August 2026 starting inventory confirmed at 387,387 t.
