What does the "Outright Vol" column represent? Direct Directional Contracts (Lots)

Outright Vol displays the absolute number of contracts traded as direct single-month outright orders (market participants taking direct directional price exposure) rather than spread roll orders. On low volume days, outright share percentage can be deceiving; actual outright volume in lots is the true measure of directional institutional conviction.

1. The FCPO Roll Window Caveat

During roll cycles (10th–20th monthly), 90%+ of gross ΔOI is calendar spread migration. Never classify roll migration as outright new longs/shorts unless Outright Volume confirms.

2. Standing OI Percentile Context

Adding longs at >85th percentile OI represents crowded late-stage momentum with squeeze risk. Adding longs at <30th percentile represents early structural re-accumulation.

3. Quantitative "Flat" Thresholds

Defined mathematically as |ΔP| ≤ 8 MYR/t (<10th% sig) and |Net ΔOI| < 450 lots (<10th% sig). Anything beyond enters the active directional quadrants.

2. How to Distinguish Spreading / Rolling vs. New Outright Longs/Shorts

Looking only at headline Total Market ΔOI can hide massive positioning shifts when traders roll or spread across the curve. This dashboard decomposes daily contract-month data (DCP(2)) using four quantitative tests:

Test 1: Matched Cross-Curve Roll Share (%)

We sum all positive contract additions (Gross +ΔOI) and all negative contract reductions (Gross -ΔOI). Matched Roll OI = min(+ΔOI, |-ΔOI|) measures how many lots exited one month (e.g. Z26 -6,269) and simultaneously entered another month (e.g. F27 +5,401). When Roll Share exceeds 65%, the session is dominated by Curve Rolling / Spread Transfer rather than net new outright flow.

Test 2: Who Is Rolling? (Calendar Spread Delta vs. Roll Flow)

When Front/2nd Month OI falls and 3rd/4th Month OI rises:
• If the Front Calendar Spread weakens (Δ(P_near - P_far) < 0, deeper contango), Longs are rolling (selling the front leg and buying the deferred leg).
• If the Front Calendar Spread strengthens (Δ(P_near - P_far) > 0), Shorts are rolling (buying back the front leg and selling the deferred leg) or bull-spreaders are lifting the front.

Test 3: New Outright Spreads vs. Outright Directional Positions

When multiple contract months gain OI simultaneously:
• Outright Directional Flow: OI build is heavily concentrated in the benchmark c3 contract, accompanied by a large flat-price move (Price Delta Significance > 60%) and parallel curve shift.
• New Calendar Spread Creation: OI builds across both near and deferred tenors simultaneously while flat price barely moves (Price Delta Significance < 25%) or the curve slope twists (Cal Delta Significance > 60%).

Test 4: Volume Conviction / Retention Ratio (ΔOI / Volume %)

High volume with |ΔOI| / Volume < 5% indicates intraday churn / day-trader scalping where positions are opened and closed before the bell. A high |ΔOI| / Volume > 15% in a contract month confirms sticky institutional position building or liquidation held overnight.
