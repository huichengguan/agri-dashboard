Updating Dashboard to Latest CME Data

To refresh the dashboard with today's official CME cleared settlements, Open Interest, Black-76 Greeks, and CFTC COT positions, choose one of the options below:

1. Double-Click `UPDATE_OPTIONS.bat` (Recommended)

In your workspace folder, simply double-click UPDATE_OPTIONS.bat. It fetches fresh LSEG Workspace quotes, recalculates VRP and Gamma, and automatically opens the updated dashboard in your browser.

2. Enable 1-Click In-Browser Refresh (`START_OPTIONS_STUDIO.bat`)

To enable this "Update to Latest" button to refresh directly inside the browser with a single click, run START_OPTIONS_STUDIO.bat (runs a lightweight background listener on port 8055).
