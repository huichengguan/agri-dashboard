🌱 Kharif Soybean • MP & Maharashtra

Late pod maturation and harvest combining active across Malwa (Indore/Ujjain) and Vidarbha/Marathwada. Open sunny conditions accelerate bean moisture drydown down to 10-12% for safe threshing, preventing mold and pod shattering while preserving seed germination viability.

🥜 Kharif & Rabi Groundnut • Gujarat, AP & TN

Saurashtra (Rajkot/Junagadh) Kharif peanut digging in full swing; dry friable soils enable clean mechanical pod lifting and rapid windrow curing. In Southern India (Rayalaseema & Tamil Nadu), late Kharif pods maturing while land prep starts for irrigated Rabi groundnut.

🌾 Rabi Rapeseed-Mustard • Rajasthan, UP & Haryana

Pre-sowing irrigation (Rauni/Palewa) underway across Alwar, Bharatpur, Morena, and South Haryana. Daytime temperatures moderating below 33°C create optimal soil thermal conditions for uniform seedbed germination and robust cotyledon establishment.
