🌱 Soybeans: The Safrinha & Wheat Squeeze

Cerrado (MT/GO/PR) rushes soybean sowing in September/October to ensure early harvest and safe planting of second-crop corn before late-season rains shut down. In Rio Grande do Sul & Argentina, 35–50% of soybeans wait for November/December wheat combines.

🌽 Corn: Safra (Verão) vs Safrinha (Tardío)

1st Crop Safra is planted now (Sep–Oct) in Southern Brazil (RS/SC/PR) and Argentina for high-yield grain and silage. 2nd Crop Safrinha represents 75%+ of Brazilian corn output, planted in Jan/Feb immediately following soybean harvest.

🌾 Wheat: South America's Winter Cereal Engine

Planted in autumn/winter (May–July), wheat is currently in its peak heading, grain filling, and harvest phase across South America. Northern PR & Paraguay are harvesting now, while Rio Grande do Sul and southern Buenos Aires head towards November/December harvest.
