🌱 AGRONOMIC TOPSOIL MOISTURE BENCHMARKS & PLANTING SEASON IMPACTS
🟣 Saturated / Waterlogged (≥ 0.40 m³/m³) Field machinery stalled; heavy tractor rutting & severe compaction. Sowing suspended; high seed rot, seedling anoxia (loss of oxygen), and damping-off fungus risks for early soybeans and corn.
🟩 Optimal Emergence (0.25 – 0.38 m³/m³) Ideal seedbed hydration for rapid radicle protrusion, uniform germination stand, and active aerobic root nodulation. High tractor trafficability with minimal compaction.
🟧 Seedbed Deficit / Dry Halt (< 0.22 m³/m³) Soil moisture insufficient for uniform emergence. Planting suspended in hot Cerrado Latosols awaiting regular soaking monsoon rains to avoid seed desiccation in hot topsoils.
